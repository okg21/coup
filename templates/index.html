Action Guide

Income: Take 1 coin from the treasury
Foreign Aid: Take 2 coins (can be blocked by Duke)
Tax: Take 3 coins (requires Duke)
Steal: Take 2 coins from another player (requires Captain, can be blocked by Ambassador/Captain)
Assassinate: Pay 3 coins to eliminate one influence from a player (requires Assassin, can be blocked by Contessa)
Coup: Pay 7 coins to eliminate one influence from a player (cannot be blocked)
Exchange: Exchange cards with the deck (requires Ambassador)
